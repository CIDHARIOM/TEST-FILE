1 Information We Collect

ANDOS collects only the essential personal information required to authenticate user accounts, complete digital fulfillments, and prevent fraudulent transactions:

  • Direct Account Identifiers: Name, verified email address, phone number, and encrypted password hash.
  • Transactional Metadata: Historical purchases, order identifiers, product SKUs, digital gift keys, delivery timestamps, and settlement amounts.
  • Financial & Payment Data: Payment details are securely processed via certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (including Stripe, Paytm, UPI networks). ANDOS does not store or process raw CVV numbers or full cardholder details on our servers.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser engine variants, device models, operating systems, and localized network latency diagnostics.
  • Inquiry & Support Communications: Transcripts of interactions with our support desk, help tickets, and verification correspondences.

2 How We Use Your Information

Every piece of data collected is processed strictly under legal, legitimate, and contractual bases:

  • Fulfillment & Delivery: To instantly generate, deliver, and record purchased digital codes, gaming top-ups, and subscription vouchers.
  • Transactional Messaging: To dispatch automated order confirmations, delivery receipts, balance alerts, and security alerts.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: To monitor anomalous purchasing patterns, mitigate automated bot operations, and protect buyer wallets.
  • Product Improvements: To understand feature usage across our digital categories and streamline user checkout journeys.
  • Statutory Compliance: To adhere to Indian financial accounting regulations, tax reporting, and lawful government requests.

3 Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

STRICT ZERO-SALE DATA PLEDGE

ANDOS does not sell, monetize, or rent your personal data to third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing brokers. Never has, never will.

We share limited information solely with trusted infrastructure service partners necessary for service execution:

  • Payment Processors: Regulated payment gateways and UPI aggregators to execute billing authorization securely.
  • Cloud & CDN Infrastructure: Enterprise-grade hosting providers and content delivery networks with strict confidentiality and encryption pacts.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where compelled by valid subpoena, court order, or statutory enforcement from Indian judicial authorities.

4 Data Security & Encryption Architecture

We deploy bank-grade technological defenses to secure customer records across their entire lifecycle:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All incoming and outgoing data transit is encrypted via 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol standards.
  • PCI-DSS Compliance: All payment flows conform to Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards.
  • Access Governance: Internal database access is restricted by granular least-privilege security tiers and multifactor authentication.
  • Threat Detection: Real-time heuristic anomaly detection, rate-limiting shields, and intrusion mitigation software.

5 Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Under applicable data protection frameworks, you are entitled to exercise comprehensive sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete export of your personal information stored within our databases.
  • Right to Rectification: Promptly correct or update any inaccurate or obsolete profile information.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Request the permanent deletion of your account and personal identifiers, subject to legal auditing obligations.
  • Consent Revocation: Opt out of non-essential promotional correspondence with a single click.

HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS

To request data erasure or an archive export, simply contact our Data Protection Officer at [email protected]. Requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.

6 Data Retention Policy

We preserve personal information only as long as necessary to satisfy operational, security, and statutory requirements:

  • Active Accounts: User profile credentials remain stored for the active duration of the user account.
  • Financial & Tax Records: Transaction logs, GST invoices, and settlement records are retained for seven (7) years in compliance with statutory Indian fiscal governance.
  • Transient Telemetry: Debug telemetry, server connection logs, and ephemeral session tokens are automatically rotated and purged after 90 days.

7 Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

Cookies and browser local storage mechanisms enhance your browsing speed and session security on ANDOS:

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Critical for user login persistence, shopping session state, and CSRF protection tokens.
  • Preferences Cookies: Retain dark/light aesthetic choices, region filters, and visual preferences.
  • Performance & Diagnostics Cookies: Anonymized metrics on page response timings and interface performance.

Managing Cookies: You can manage, block, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Note that disabling essential cookies may impact account sign-in functionality.

8 Minor Protection & Age Policy

ANDOS does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from individuals under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If we discover that personal data from a minor has been gathered without parental authorization, we will expeditiously expunge that information from our active records.

9 Policy Updates & Notifications

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect emerging legal standards, technical improvements, or operational evolutions. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date". In the event of material alterations affecting your rights, we will provide prominent notification across the platform.
